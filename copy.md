## 7. CASE STUDY — topstartups.lat
**Problem:** A Latin American startup directory looked finished but wasn't performing. The founders suspected SEO issues.

**Three key findings:**
- Every canonical, social, and sitemap URL pointed to localhost:3100 — the dev-server address had leaked into production.
- The sitemap returned a 404 page, so Google couldn't discover new pages.
- The directory was empty: all 24 country filters showed zero listings, and one homepage section rendered completely blank.

**Outcome:** We shipped the findings as a ranked fix roadmap — one config line fixes the URL problem, the rest sequenced by impact.
